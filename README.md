Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Word From Grandma](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xkianno-one-word-from-grandma-f04bed16).

The bounty asks for a single word that captures a grandmother. This entry's
word is **eat** — the first thing every grandma says when you walk through
her door, before hello, before how-are-you. It is her greeting, her love
language, and her standing order, all in three letters.

Running `main.py` writes the word to `entry.txt` in `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
(falling back to `/output`).
